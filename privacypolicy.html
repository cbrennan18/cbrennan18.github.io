Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how cbrennan.ie (“the site”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you browse pages like projects, visualisations, and interactive demos. We aim to keep data collection minimal and transparent.

Key points at a glance
  • We do not use Google Analytics.
  • We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for privacy-friendly, aggregated usage metrics (no cookies).
  • Some pages embed third-party content (e.g., Tableau public charts) which may set their own cookies.
  • The Spotify page uses Spotify’s OAuth to fetch your stats in your browser; we don’t store or receive your token server-side.

Information we collect

Minimal, aggregated usage data. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand basic site usage (page views, referrers, device/viewport info, etc.). These metrics are reported in aggregate form and are not tied to your identity. Cloudflare’s script does not use cookies.

When you contact us. If you reach out via the site’s contact option (e.g., the footer modal or by email), we’ll receive the information you provide (such as your name, email address, and message) solely to respond to you. We don’t sell or share this information.

Third-party content & embeds

Some pages embed or load content from third parties:

  • Tableau Public (e.g., the Netflix analysis page) to display interactive charts. These embeds load from public.tableau.com and may set their own cookies or collect usage information under their privacy policies. We don’t control their data practices.
  • CDNs for assets such as Bootstrap, AOS, Chart.js, and fonts. When your browser requests these files from their CDNs, the providers receive standard request information (e.g., IP address, user agent).

We include links to external sites (e.g., Unsplash photo credits). Visiting those sites is subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

Spotify page specifics

The Spotify analysis page lets you visualise your own listening data using the official Spotify Web API:

  • You are redirected to Spotify to grant permission (OAuth). Spotify then redirects you back with an access token in the URL fragment.
  • The access token is used client-side only (in your browser) to fetch your top tracks/artists and audio features directly from Spotify’s API.
  • We do not receive, log, or store your access token or your Spotify data on our server.
  • When you close or reload the page, the token is cleared from the URL.

Cookies & local storage

First-party cookies: The site does not set analytics or marketing cookies. Interactive pages (e.g., tables/charts) may use in-browser state (like memory) to render results but do not persist personal data.

Cloudflare Web Analytics: Used for aggregated metrics and does not use cookies.

Third-party embeds (e.g., Tableau): May set their own cookies. You can control third-party cookies via your browser settings. If you prefer not to load embedded content, you can browse with script-blocking tools or disable third-party cookies.

Data retention

We don’t maintain user profiles. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry, and then deleted or archived securely.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the site and any information you provide. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Your choices

  • Use a modern browser to block third-party cookies if you don’t want embeds to set them.
  • You can choose not to authorise the Spotify page; the page will not access your Spotify data without your consent.
  • Contact us if you want any message you’ve sent removed from our records.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect site changes or legal requirements. Material updates will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions? Reach out via the site’s Contact Form (linked in the footer) or by email if provided there.
